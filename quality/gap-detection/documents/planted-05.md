# Notification requirements

## Purpose

Tell a member about work that needs their attention without waking them.

## Rules

1. A member receives a notification when they are mentioned or assigned.
2. Notifications are delivered by email and by mobile push.
3. Notifications are not delivered during quiet hours, which run from 22:00
   to 07:00.
4. A notification held during quiet hours is delivered when they end.
5. A member may turn off notifications for a single channel.

## Preferences

The preferences screen offers one switch that turns notifications off for the
member across every channel.

## Out of scope

Digest emails and workspace-wide announcements.
