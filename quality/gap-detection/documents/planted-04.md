# Sales reporting requirements

## Purpose

Give the sales team a weekly view of closed revenue.

## Rules

1. A report covers one calendar week, Monday through Sunday.
2. A report lists closed deals, their value, and the account that closed them.
3. A report is generated every Monday at 06:00 in the account time zone.
4. A generated report is kept for 90 days and then deleted.
5. A member of the sales team may export a report as a spreadsheet.

## Retention

Exported spreadsheets and generated reports are retained for one year so that
the year-end review can reproduce any week.

## Figures

The revenue figure shown in the report is the figure the business reports
externally.

## Out of scope

Forecasting and commission calculation.
