# Billing trial requirements

## Purpose

Let a new team try the paid plan before it is charged.

## Rules

1. A new workspace starts a free trial when its first member signs up.
2. The free trial lasts 14 days from the sign-up date.
3. During the trial the workspace may invite an unlimited number of members.
4. When the trial ends the workspace is charged for its active users.
5. A workspace that adds a payment method before the trial ends keeps the
   same billing date.

## Reporting

Finance receives a monthly summary of trial conversions. The summary counts
every workspace whose 30-day trial converted to a paid plan.

## Out of scope

Discounts, annual plans, and tax handling.
